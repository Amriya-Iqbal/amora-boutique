AMORA was founded on a singular conviction: that garments should not merely decorate our days, but elevate them with enduring beauty, intention, and tactile pleasure.

OUR BEGINNINGS

The Art of Mindful Couture

Born in our Colombo design studio, AMORA represents an antidote to the frantic pace of modern fast fashion. We create small-batch seasonal capsules characterized by fluid drapes, tailored structure, and tactile luxury.

Every pattern is draped by hand, refined through multiple fitting rounds, and realized exclusively in certified natural or sustainably recycled fibers. We celebrate clothes that outlast seasons and become treasured heirlooms.

OUR CORE PILLARS

The AMORA Standard

Three guiding commitments embedded in every garment we produce.

Ethical Fibers

We source certified French flax linen, OEKO-TEX® certified silks, and organic cottons from responsible mills with fair-wage guarantees.

Architectural Fit

Designed to complement female geometry with bias-cut fluidity, French interior seams, and contouring waistlines engineered for grace.

Generational Longevity

Pieces constructed with reinforced stress seams and double-lined bodices so they retain shape, texture, and luminosity year after year.
